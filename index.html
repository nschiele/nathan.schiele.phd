Nathan Daniel Schiele

Doctoral candidate, Leiden Institute of Advanced Computer Science (LIACS), Leiden University.

I work on attack-defense trees, and more broadly on whether graphical threat models survive contact with the people who have to use them. My thesis, Attack-Defense Trees: A Practical Threat Model, asks three questions: where attack-defense trees fit alongside other graphical models such as attack graphs; what makes an attack tree a good attack tree; and whether these models are usable by the full range of stakeholders who make security decisions, including the non-technical ones.
